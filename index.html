About Christina

Hi there,

I am Christina, a women's holistic bodyworker based in Newcastle, NSW.

My former midwifery career gifted me great insight into women's experiences in pregnancy, birth and the postnatal period. I came to understand that pregnancy can be a deeply transformative time and also incredibly challenging. I frequently encountered women experiencing emotional and psychological overwhelm in navigating these big changes as well as the musculoskeletal pain that can arise with pregnancy and post birth.

Working in the busy hospital system ignited my desire to have the space and time to support all women in a gentle, holistic and intuitive way. One Earth Alchemy is how I offer women the connection, tenderness and care they deserve through life's big transitions. I offer patience and presence. My massage treatments are deeply intentional and nurturing, designed to ease the tension and discomfort that can come with bringing a baby into the world.

I draw on my remedial massage therapy and midwifery experience to ensure that all treatments are safe, comprehensive and that women leave feeling understood and honoured in their journey.

Relaxation Massage

Surrender and release with this deeply restorative treatment. It is slow, peaceful and flowing. Centred around calming and soothing the nervous system to allow you to feel safe and at ease within your own body and mind. Pressure is always adapted to your desire to nourish and nurture you even more. Return to your breathe, your body and inner harmony.

Remedial Massage

This treatment is more clinical in nature, designed to release the root cause of pain and discomfort in the body. It incorporates deep tissue massage, trigger point release, stretching and passive mobilisation to alleviate the areas where you hold most tension. Bring your body back to balance, restore homeostasis. Move with ease, grace and confidence.
